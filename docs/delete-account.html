Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Forage for All account and all data associated with it at any time. No questions, no retention tricks.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Your profile (handle, display name, badges)
  • Every pin you created
  • Your ripeness reports and comments
  • Your saved spots

Deletion completes within 30 days of the request. Nothing is retained afterward.

How to request it

  1. In the app: Profile → Delete account (brings you to this page), or
  2. By email: send a message from your account email with the subject "Delete my account":

Email a deletion request

We verify the request by matching the sender address to the account email — that's all magic-code accounts have, so it's the strongest verification available.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You don't need to delete your account to remove content: any pin, ripeness report, or comment you created can be deleted directly in the app, immediately and permanently.

Good to know: browsing the map never required an account in the first place — accounts exist only so the community can trust who's confirming what's ripe. Read the full privacy policy.
